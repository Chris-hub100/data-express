Terms of Service

Last Updated: February 2025

1. Hello & Welcome

Welcome to DataExpress. We built this platform to make campus life easier by connecting you to data, food, and entertainment. By using our site, you're agreeing to these simple rules designed to keep the service running smoothly for everyone.

2. The Food Run (Logistics)

Our weekend food delivery is a batch-based service. Here’s the deal:

  • Service Hours: We operate from Friday 4:00 PM to Sunday 10:00 PM.
  • The 10-Minute Rule: Our dispatchers have a lot of rooms to cover. They will wait for a maximum of 10 minutes at your hostel/hall. If you don't show up, the order is forfeited to keep the run moving.
  • No Returns: Since it's food, we can't take it back once it's been picked up from the vendor. Please check your package immediately.
3. Data Bundles & Digital Tools

We provide instant top-ups, but accuracy is on you:

  • Check Your Number: Double-check the recipient number. Data sent to the wrong number cannot be reversed or refunded.
  • Processing Time: Most bundles land in seconds. If there's a network delay, please wait up to 30 minutes before contacting support.
  • DStv/ECG: Make sure your Smartcard or Meter ID is correct. Once the payment hits the utility provider, we can't pull it back.
4. Payments & Your Security

We take your money's safety seriously:

  • Secure Processing: All payments are handled by regulated partner gateways (like Hubtel). We never see or store your MoMo PIN or card details.
  • Failed Payments: If your money is deducted but the service doesn't deliver, our system logs these errors and we usually resolve them within 24 hours.
5. DataExpress tv+ (Third-Party Content)

Our entertainment hub is a curation service designed for your convenience:

  • Content Hosting: DataExpress does **not** host, upload, or own any of the video files shown. All movie trailers are embedded directly from YouTube using their official API/Player.
  • Intellectual Property: All movie trailers, posters, and trademarks belong to their respective creators and film studios. We simply index this public content to help you decide what to watch.
  • External Links: If you click a "Play Now" link that takes you off our site, the terms of that third-party site apply.
6. Student Conduct

DataExpress is a community tool. Any attempt to use the platform for fraudulent activities, MoMo scams, or harassing our dispatchers will result in an immediate permanent ban from all our services.
