Ledgehold Ghana's DataExpress Enterprise Standards

Commitment to Act 843

DataExpress operates in strict accordance with the Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana. We treat Student ID data as "Special Personal Data," ensuring maximum encryption and limited access.

What we see
  • Full Name & University Details
  • Course of Study
  • MoMo Payout Numbers
  • Daily Logs & Receipts
How we protect it
  • AES-256 Cloud Encryption
  • 48-hour Auto-Purge for IDs
  • No Third-Party Data Selling
  • Encrypted Hubtel Handshakes
The Gatekeeper Policy

To maintain trust with the **Bank of Ghana** and audit partners, we require proof of delivery for every payout request submitted. This data is used solely to protect marketplace margins.

Third-Party Disclosure

We use existing, enterprise tools to minimize OpEx. Your data is routed through ImgBB (Storage) and Hubtel (Payments). These providers are industry-standard and maintain their own rigorous security protocols.
